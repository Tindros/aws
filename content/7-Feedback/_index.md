> Here, I would like to share some personal feedback and comments based on my experience participating in the First Cloud AI Journey program. The following comments are based on my actual experience during the internship and are intended to contribute to improving the program for future interns.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

The working environment within the First Cloud AI Journey program was friendly and open. I had opportunities to work and communicate with other members of the program, which made me feel more comfortable asking questions and sharing issues I encountered. Activities at the office and events organized by the program also gave me opportunities to learn more about the actual working environment.

**2. Support from Mentors / Team Admin**

The mentors and team admin provided support throughout my internship, especially when I encountered issues or concepts that I did not fully understand during my learning and project development. The guidance and explanations helped me become more proactive in researching and solving problems instead of relying entirely on predefined instructions.

**3. Relevance of the Work to My Major**

The internship activities were relevant to my Data Science major and my technical development goals. In addition to the knowledge I had learned at university, the program allowed me to gain exposure to AWS Cloud, Serverless Architecture, and the process of building and deploying an application in a practical environment. This helped me better understand how fundamental knowledge can be applied to real-world projects.

**4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities**

During the program, I had the opportunity to acquire new knowledge and skills, particularly in AWS Cloud and services such as Amazon Cognito, AWS Lambda, Amazon API Gateway, Amazon DynamoDB, IAM, and Amazon S3. In addition to technical knowledge, I also improved my ability to independently research documentation, manage source code using Git/GitHub, write technical documentation, and present my work.

Participating in activities and events organized by the program also gave me a broader perspective on the Cloud and AI industry and helped me better understand the working environment in the technology field.

**5. Culture and Teamwork**

I appreciated the spirit of support and knowledge sharing within the program. Having opportunities to communicate with other interns and members allowed me to learn from different approaches and perspectives. The group activities also provided opportunities for members to connect and contributed to a more positive internship experience.

**6. Policies and Support for Interns**

The program provided interns with opportunities to learn, explore, and practice knowledge related to AWS. Access to learning materials, workshops, and community activities was a valuable aspect of the program, providing additional resources for developing knowledge and skills.

### Other Questions

- **What did I enjoy the most during the internship?**

  What I enjoyed the most was the opportunity to gain hands-on experience with AWS Cloud through learning and developing a practical project. Independently researching, building, and deploying the **Serverless Personal Expense Tracker** helped me better understand the process of developing an application on a cloud platform.

- **What do I think the program could improve for future interns?**

  I think the program could provide more hands-on guidance sessions for each stage of the project, especially for the deployment and integration of AWS services. In addition, more experience-sharing sessions with professionals currently working in the Cloud and AI fields would give interns a better understanding of real-world work and career development.

- **Would I recommend this program to my friends? Why?**

  Yes, I would recommend the program to students who are interested in learning about AWS Cloud, Cloud Computing, or related AI technologies. The program provides opportunities to gain practical knowledge, work with AWS services, and experience a professional working environment while encouraging self-learning and proactive exploration.

### Suggestions and Expectations

- **Suggestions for improving the internship experience:**

  I suggest that the program could organize more hands-on workshops or group activities in which each group builds a small project from start to finish. This would give interns more opportunities to apply their knowledge, exchange ideas, and develop teamwork skills.

- **Would I like to continue participating in the program in the future?**

  I hope the First Cloud AI Journey program will continue and develop in the coming years. If given the opportunity, I would also like to participate in future community activities, workshops, or learning programs related to AWS and Cloud Computing.

- **Other comments:**

  I would like to express my sincere thanks to the First Cloud AI Journey team, mentors, and members who supported me throughout my internship. This was a valuable experience that helped me gain knowledge of AWS Cloud, better understand the process of developing and deploying a practical project, and recognize the skills that I need to continue improving in the future.
